01 / Why it matters

Most websites are silently bleeding new customers every single day.

// 01

Trust is decided in 4 seconds.

Before someone reads a word, they've already decided whether your business looks legit. A polished, professional-looking site says "this is a real operator." A clunky one says "I'll keep scrolling." That four-second read is costing you customers you'll never even know existed.

// 02

9 in 10 visitors are on a phone.

If your site is hard to read on a phone — text too small, buttons too fiddly, photos cut off — they're already on the next result. We build mobile-first, so the experience is sharp on the device almost all of your visitors are actually using.

// 03

The bar has moved.

What looked fine back in 2022 looks tired today. Customers now compare you against the slickest sites they've used all week — banks, retailers, big brands. A site that looks five years old quietly tells them your business probably is too. A modern site closes that gap.

02 / The pitch

What if your site won you one extra customer a week? Just one.

The maths is brutally simple. And in your favour.

Most small business websites aren't broken — they're just not professional. Outdated layouts, mismatched fonts, photos pulled from a phone in 2017, designs that haven't aged well. The contact form works. The phone number is there. None of that matters if a new customer's first impression is "this looks a bit dodgy."

A polished, professional-looking website gives a first-time visitor the confidence to actually enquire. That single shift — looking like a serious operator instead of a part-timer — is what turns browsers into paying customers.

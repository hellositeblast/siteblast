Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including your name, email address, phone number, and business details when you enquire about or use our services. We also collect usage data and analytics from websites we manage on your behalf.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve our services, communicate with you about your account, send you invoices and receipts, and where you have opted in, send you service updates and tips.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share information with service providers who assist us in delivering our services (e.g. hosting providers, payment processors). All third parties are required to handle your data in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Your Rights

Under the Australian Privacy Act 1988, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. Contact us at hello@siteblast.com.au to exercise these rights.

6. Cookies

Our website uses cookies for analytics and to improve user experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect functionality.

7. Contact

For privacy-related enquiries, contact us at hello@siteblast.com.au.
